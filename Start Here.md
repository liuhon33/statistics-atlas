# Statistics atlas

New here? Open [[README|the illustrated setup and usage guide]] for installation, screenshots, agents, and the included skills.

Open **Statistics atlas: Open statistics atlas** from the command palette, or select the network icon in the ribbon.

- **Backbone** shows the reference-topic hierarchy and typed connections. The 2026-11-09 snapshot has 757 topics and 845 backbone connections.
- **My learning** adds personal notes and selected connections. The snapshot includes 14 learning notes.
- **Notes only** shows personal notes and links between them, keeping their map positions.
- **Everything** adds source records and administrative material. Use **Fit visible** to include the outer auxiliary cluster.

Search a title or alias to focus a branch. Select a dot to inspect its path and connections; double-click a dot or choose **Open note** to open its page. Zoom with the wheel, drag to pan, and use **Whole web** to return.

The map reads note properties. Plain-text breadcrumbs do not create edges. Backbone positions are frozen across view switches and personal-note additions.
