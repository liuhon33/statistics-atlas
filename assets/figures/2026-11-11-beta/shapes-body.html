A distribution for an unknown probability

Imagine a coin with unknown chance p of heads. The curve describes uncertainty about p, not the two outcomes of a flip.

Fixed axes: p from 0 to 1; density from 0 to 6. The dashed vertical line marks the mean. Curves above the ceiling are clipped and marked ↑. Interior sampling is denser near the edges. Infinite endpoint limits are not probability masses. Each full density has area 1, even if part is clipped.
