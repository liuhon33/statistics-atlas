Exact formulas apply to iid normal observations with variance 1. This is a finite-sample comparison, not a proof of the general CLT or delta theorem. Keeping the same z as n changes couples one-time examples; it does not simulate a nested growing dataset. The distribution panel averages over all possible Z and is independent of the selected z.
