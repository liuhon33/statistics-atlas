What are the points and units?

Each point is one sample with two readings in the same measurement units. Column means (10, 20) have already been removed. The plot has equal horizontal and vertical scales. Scores have measurement units; variance and squared error have squared units. The sample variance divisor is n − 1 = 11.

The direction is v = (cos θ, sin θ). Each score is t = x · v and its reconstructed point is t v. This original deterministic example is the one used throughout the note.
